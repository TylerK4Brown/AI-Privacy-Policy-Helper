# Purpose

This folder allows you to copy-paste some sample privacy policy texts that were selected by us during this study. We determined that these privacy policies were overall difficult to navigate or overwhelming at first glance, and they served as great test prompts for our AI Privacy Policy Helper.

To view the privacy policy texts in their original form, please view the links below.

# Links to each privacy policy

**Cisco**: https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/about/legal/privacy-full.html

**Lenovo**: https://www.lenovo.com/us/en/privacy/ (we focused on the "What information does Lenovo collect; how is the information used; and is any personal information shared with third parties?" section.)
